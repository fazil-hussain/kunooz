Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kunooz Al Asalah Building Maintenance L.L.C. ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information in connection with this website and our services in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Information we collect

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts or online payments, and it does not ask you to enter personal information. We only receive personal information when you choose to contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. This may include your name, phone number, email address, property address and details of the work you need.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and give you a quotation.
  • To arrange site visits and carry out the requested work.
  • To issue invoices and keep business records as required by UAE law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Third-party services

This website uses a few external services, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit:

  • Google Fonts to display the website's typefaces.
  • Google Maps to show our office location on the Contact page.
  • WhatsApp when you choose to message us through a WhatsApp link.

These services handle information under their own privacy policies. Our website hosting provider may also keep standard server logs for security and maintenance.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers on this website. The third-party services listed above may use cookies under their own policies.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry and customer information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting obligations, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable UAE law, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
