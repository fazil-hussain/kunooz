Preventive Maintenance: A Simple Checklist for Villas and Apartments in Dubai

Small problems become costly repairs when they are ignored. This practical checklist helps property owners and tenants stay ahead of the most common maintenance issues.

By Kunooz Al Asalah Team·28 August 2026·6 min read

Most building repairs start as small issues: a slow drip under a sink, a hairline crack in the paint, a breaker that trips now and then. Preventive maintenance means checking these things on a regular schedule, before they turn into water damage, electrical faults or a broken AC in peak summer. Here is a simple checklist you can follow.

Monthly checks

  • AC filters: clean or replace them, especially after sandy days.
  • Under sinks: look for damp cabinet floors, drips or corrosion on pipes and connections.
  • Drains: make sure showers, basins and floor drains empty quickly and have no bad smells.
  • Smoke detectors: press the test button and replace batteries where needed.

Every three to six months

  • AC service: a professional clean of coils, drain lines and a gas pressure check.
  • Water heater: check for leaks around the tank and valve and listen for unusual noises.
  • Electrical points: look for scorched or loose sockets and switches, and flickering lights.
  • Silicone and grout: renew gaps around baths, showers and kitchen counters to stop water getting behind tiles.
  • Doors and windows: tighten hinges, lubricate locks, and check window seals to keep dust and heat out.

Once a year

  • Water tanks: have rooftop or underground tanks cleaned and disinfected.
  • Roof and terrace: inspect waterproofing, especially before the short rainy season.
  • Exterior paint: sun and humidity break down paint over time, so touch up faded or peeling areas.
  • Distribution board: have a qualified electrician inspect breakers and connections.
  • Pest control: schedule a general treatment for the whole property.

Why it is worth the effort

Regular checks reduce emergency call-outs, extend the life of equipment such as AC units and water heaters, and help protect the value of the property. For landlords, a well-maintained unit also makes handovers between tenants faster and simpler.

Keep a record: Note the date of each service and any parts replaced. It makes future troubleshooting easier and is useful when renewing a tenancy or selling a property.
