About Us

A building maintenance company based in Port Saeed, Dubai.

Who We Are

Kunooz Al Asalah Building Maintenance L.L.C.

Kunooz Al Asalah Building Maintenance L.L.C. is a limited liability company registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. From our office in the Arab Bank Building, Port Saeed, we provide building maintenance and technical services for residential and commercial properties.

Our work covers air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing and sanitary, painting, tiling and carpentry, so property owners and tenants can rely on a single company for everyday repairs and planned maintenance.

We aim to respond quickly, explain the work clearly, agree on the price before starting, and leave every site clean.

Our Approach

How We Work

1. Tell Us the Problem

Call, WhatsApp or email us with a short description and photos if possible.

2. Inspection & Quote

A technician visits, checks the issue and gives you a clear quotation.

3. Work Completed

Once you approve, we carry out the work and clean up the site afterwards.
